Star Sprite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Star Sprite is an AI game-making engine by Michael Dembenski & Bit. It is available as a web app and as a Quest app (SideQuest). This policy explains what Star Sprite does with your information.

What Star Sprite collects

Star Sprite does not require an account, a sign-in, or any personal details from you. It does not collect your name, email address, or location, and it does not show ads or sell data.

When you use Star Sprite, two kinds of content leave your device, only when you choose to use the feature:

  • Game prompts (text). When you type a game idea, the prompt is sent to the AI provider you selected in the app (Meta Muse Wonder, Groq, or the offline Star Sprite engine). The provider uses it to generate your game.
  • Voice input (audio). When you speak a game idea, your microphone audio is sent to Groq Whisper for transcription, using your own free Groq API key. The audio is used only to turn your speech into a text prompt.

What Star Sprite stores

Your API key, settings, and provider choice are stored locally on your own device only (in the app's local storage). They are never sent to us. Your generated games are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them.

What Star Sprite does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no multiplayer — Star Sprite is single-player only.
  • No tracking, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • We do not receive, store, or sell your prompts, voice audio, or API keys.

Your prompts and voice audio are processed by the AI provider you selected (Meta or Groq). Those providers have their own privacy policies, which govern how they handle the content you send them.

Children

Star Sprite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
